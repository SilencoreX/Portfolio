Privacy, in plain language.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This site is run by Sufiyan (SilencoreX) from Mumbai, India. It is a portfolio website. It does not use ads, and it does not sell or share your information.

What the review form collects

  • Name, star rating and review text. These are shown publicly on the website, together with the month the review was written.
  • Email address. Used only so I can check that feedback is genuine and reply if needed. It is stored privately and is never shown on the website.

Reviews are stored in Google Firebase (Firestore). If you want your review or email removed, email arhaansvp00@gmail.com from the same address and I will delete it.

What is stored in your browser

A few small values are saved on your device only (local storage), for example to stop duplicate reviews, to remember that you marked a review helpful, and to avoid showing the review reminder again too soon. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts loads the typeface, so your browser contacts Google when the page opens.
  • Google Firebase stores and serves reviews.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.

The apps

Premium, CodeChecker and APK Builder each have their own official page. This policy covers only this portfolio website, so please read the information on each app's page before installing.
